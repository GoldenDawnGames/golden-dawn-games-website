01 / THE GAME

A DIFFERENT KIND
OF UNO.

UNO Rush! takes the recognizable card colors and identity of UNO and transforms them into a fast, satisfying match-3 puzzle experience.

The core idea is simple.

Find matching cards and merge three cards of the same color to clear them from the board.

As players progress, levels introduce new challenges, obstacles and mechanics that require more thoughtful moves.
