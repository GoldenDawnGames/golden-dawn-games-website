01 / THE GAME

A DIFFERENT KIND
OF UNO.

UNO Rush! takes UNO-inspired cards and transforms them into a fast-paced match-3 puzzle experience.

The objective is simple.

Find matching cards and merge three cards of the same color.

Clear the board, complete the objective and move forward.

As the journey continues, new mechanics, obstacles and increasingly challenging levels keep the experience fresh.
